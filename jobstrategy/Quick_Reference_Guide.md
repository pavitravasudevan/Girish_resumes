## ⚡ Quick Decision Matrix: Should I Apply?

### ✅ APPLY if the job has:
- ✅ Location: NJ or within 1 hour commute
- ✅ Title includes: Scientist, Senior Scientist, Principal Scientist
- ✅ Keywords: Process Development, Downstream, Upstream, CPD, Biologics
- ✅ Company: Any on your Tier 1 or Tier 2 list
- ✅ Requirements: Match 70%+ of your experience

### ⚠️ MAYBE (research more) if:
- ⚠️ Location: 1-2 hour commute (evaluate remote/hybrid options)
- ⚠️ Company: Small biotech (<500 employees) with no products yet
- ⚠️ Title: "Associate Scientist" (may be too junior)
- ⚠️ Requirements: Match 50-70% of your experience

### ❌ SKIP if:
- ❌ Location: >2 hours from NJ with no remote option
- ❌ Focus: Non-biologics (small molecules, formulation only)
- ❌ Title: "Technician", "Associate" (unless seeking entry level)
- ❌ Requirements: PhD required but you have MS (unless it says "or equivalent experience")
- ❌ Posted: >6 months ago (likely already filled)

## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

### During Job Search:
- ❌ Job reposted multiple times (may indicate retention issues)
- ❌ Glassdoor reviews consistently mention poor management
- ❌ Company has recent layoffs (search "[Company] layoffs 2026")
- ❌ Unrealistic requirements (10+ years for "entry level")

### During Interview:
- ❌ Interviewers speak negatively about the company
- ❌ High turnover mentioned casually
- ❌ Vague answers about career growth
- ❌ Pressure to accept offer quickly without time to consider
- ❌ Unwilling to discuss work-life balance

### Green Flags:
- ✅ Interviewers enthusiastic about their work
- ✅ Clear career progression paths described
- ✅ Work-life balance mentioned proactively
- ✅ Team stability (people have been there 5+ years)
- ✅ Investment in employee development
