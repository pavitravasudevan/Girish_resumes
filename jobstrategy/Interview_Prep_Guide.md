## 🔬 Technical Knowledge Review

### Key Concepts to Review

#### Downstream Processing
- [ ] Chromatography principles (IEX, HIC, AC, SEC)
- [ ] Ultrafiltration/Diafiltration (UF/DF)
- [ ] Viral clearance (low pH, nanofiltration, UV)
- [ ] Depth filtration
- [ ] Process economics and yield optimization
- [ ] Column packing and qualification
- [ ] Buffer preparation and formulation

#### Upstream Processing
- [ ] Cell line development
- [ ] Fed-batch vs perfusion culture
- [ ] Bioreactor design and operation
- [ ] Media optimization
- [ ] Process analytical technology (PAT)
- [ ] Cell culture troubleshooting

#### Analytical Methods
- [ ] HPLC (RP-HPLC, SEC-HPLC)
- [ ] Capillary electrophoresis (CE-SDS, iCE)
- [ ] Mass spectrometry basics
- [ ] ELISA and bioassays
- [ ] Endotoxin testing
- [ ] Aggregation analysis

#### Quality & Compliance
- [ ] GMP/cGMP regulations
- [ ] ICH guidelines
- [ ] Process validation (IQ/OQ/PQ)
- [ ] Risk assessment (FMEA)
- [ ] Deviation management
- [ ] Documentation requirements

## 🗣️ Questions to Ask the Interviewer

### About the Role
1. What does a typical day/week look like in this position?
2. What are the immediate priorities for this role in the first 3-6 months?
3. What processes/molecules would I be working with?
4. What stage of development are the programs in (early research, clinical, commercial)?
5. What's the team structure and who would I collaborate with most?

### About the Team & Culture
1. How would you describe the team culture and collaboration style?
2. What opportunities are there for professional development and training?
3. How does the company support work-life balance?
4. What makes someone successful in this role?

### About Projects & Technology
1. What technologies and platforms does the team currently use?
2. Are there opportunities to work on innovative/cutting-edge projects?
3. How does the company approach process innovation vs. proven methods?
4. What's the tech transfer process like to manufacturing?

### About Career Growth
1. What career progression opportunities exist within the organization?
2. How does the company support continued learning and development?
3. Are there opportunities to present at conferences or publish?

## 💪 STAR Method Template

**Situation**: Set the context
**Task**: Describe the challenge or responsibility
**Action**: Explain what YOU did specifically
**Result**: Share the measurable outcome

### Example STAR Response

**Q: Describe a time you improved a process.**

**S**: At [Company], our downstream purification process had low yield (~60%) for our lead mAb candidate.

**T**: I was tasked with identifying bottlenecks and improving overall recovery to >80% while maintaining purity.

**A**: I conducted a systematic study using DOE to optimize each chromatography step. I identified that the Protein A step was causing product loss due to harsh elution conditions. I developed a gentler elution protocol and optimized the pH range.

**R**: The improved process achieved 85% yield while maintaining >99% purity, saving ~$2M annually in production costs and accelerating clinical supply timelines by 3 months.
