## Pfizer

### Company Overview
- **Headquarters**: New York, NY
- **NJ Locations**: Pearl River, NY (close to NJ border, ~30 min from North NJ)
- **Global Employees**: ~83,000
- **Focus Areas**: Vaccines, Oncology, Rare Diseases, Inflammation & Immunology, Internal Medicine
- **Manufacturing Capabilities**: Large-scale biologics manufacturing, mRNA vaccine production
- **Recent News**:
  - Leader in mRNA vaccine technology post-COVID
  - Acquired Seagen (2023) for $43B - expanding oncology ADC portfolio
  - Strong oncology and rare disease pipeline

### Culture & Values
- **Core Values**: Courage, Excellence, Equity, Joy
- **Known For**: Science-driven, patient-focused, collaborative environment
- **Work-Life Balance**: Generally good; flexible work arrangements
- **Career Development**: Strong internal mobility, extensive training programs
- **DEI Commitment**: Active diversity and inclusion initiatives

### Interview Process
- **Typical Timeline**: 4-6 weeks from application to offer
- **Rounds**:
  1. Phone screen with recruiter (30 min)
  2. Technical interview with hiring manager (60 min)
  3. Panel interview with team members (2-3 hours, 4-5 people)
  4. Final interview with senior leadership (optional for senior roles)
- **Focus Areas**: Technical depth, problem-solving, teamwork, patient impact
- **Prep Tips**: Know your technical expertise deeply, prepare STAR examples

### Key Products/Pipeline
- **Blockbusters**: Comirnaty (COVID vaccine), Paxlovid, Eliquis, Ibrance, Prevnar
- **Oncology**: ADCs (from Seagen acquisition), Lorbrena, Talzenna
- **Rare Disease**: Vyndaqel, gene therapies
- **Pipeline**: 110+ programs in development, focus on oncology and rare diseases

### Questions to Ask
1. What therapeutic areas/molecules would I be working on in this role?
2. How does Pfizer approach tech transfer from development to commercial manufacturing?
3. What opportunities exist to work across different modalities (small molecules, biologics, mRNA)?
4. How is the team structured between Pearl River and other sites?
5. What's Pfizer's strategy for integrating Seagen's ADC platform?

https://pfizer.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/PfizerCareers
