---

## Standard Cover Letter Format

**[Your Name]**
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Your Email]
[Your Phone]
[Date]

[Hiring Manager Name - if available]
[Title]
[Company Name]
[Company Address]

Dear [Hiring Manager Name / Hiring Committee],

**PARAGRAPH 1: Opening & Hook**
I am writing to express my strong interest in the [Job Title] position at [Company Name], as advertised on [where you found it]. With [X years] of experience in [specific relevant area like "downstream process development for monoclonal antibodies" or "cell culture optimization for biologics manufacturing"], I am excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Company Name]'s mission to [mention specific company goal/product/mission].

**PARAGRAPH 2: Why You're a Great Fit (Technical Match)**
In my current/previous role at [Company], I have [specific achievement related to job description]. For example:
- [Specific accomplishment with numbers]: "Optimized a 3-step chromatography process that increased yield by 25% while maintaining >99% purity"
- [Relevant skill application]: "Led tech transfer of 2 mAb processes from development to commercial manufacturing"
- [Technical expertise]: "Developed and validated viral clearance strategies using low pH treatment and nanofiltration"

My expertise in [list 3-4 key skills from job description] directly aligns with the requirements outlined in the job posting.

**PARAGRAPH 3: Why This Company/Role (Show You've Done Research)**
I am particularly drawn to [Company Name] because of [specific reason - could be pipeline, technology, culture, recent news]. I was impressed by [specific achievement/product/initiative of the company], and I am eager to contribute to [specific project or goal mentioned in job description]. The opportunity to work on [mention specific platform/molecule/therapeutic area if known] aligns perfectly with my career goals and passion for [relevant area].

**PARAGRAPH 4: Closing & Call to Action**
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in [key skill area] and track record of [specific achievement type] can contribute to [Company Name]'s continued success. I am confident that my [key strengths] make me an excellent fit for this role.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of discussing this exciting opportunity with you further. Please feel free to contact me at [phone] or [email] to schedule a conversation.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]

---

## 📝 Customization Checklist

Before sending each cover letter, ensure you've:
- [ ] Researched the hiring manager's name (check LinkedIn, company website)
- [ ] Mentioned specific skills from the job description
- [ ] Included 2-3 quantifiable achievements
- [ ] Referenced something specific about the company (recent news, product, mission)
- [ ] Matched the tone to the company culture (formal vs. more casual)
- [ ] Proofread multiple times (no typos!)
- [ ] Kept it to one page maximum
- [ ] Saved as PDF with clear filename: "Girish_LastName_CoverLetter_CompanyName.pdf"

The opportunity to work in Sanofi's state-of-the-art Bridgewater facility and contribute to life-changing therapies aligns perfectly with my passion for advancing biopharmaceutical manufacturing.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my technical skills and collaborative approach can contribute to Sanofi's continued leadership in biologics development. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of speaking with you soon.

Sincerely,

Girish [Last Name]

## Quick Tips for Different Roles

### For Downstream Process Development
**Emphasize**:
- Chromatography expertise (Protein A, IEX, HIC, etc.)
- Viral clearance experience
- UF/DF optimization
- Analytical method development
- Scale-up experience

### For Upstream/CPD Roles
**Emphasize**:
- Cell culture experience (CHO, HEK293, etc.)
- Bioreactor operation and optimization
- Media/feed development
- Process analytical technology (PAT)
- Clone selection and cell line development

### For Tech Transfer/Manufacturing Science
**Emphasize**:
- GMP experience
- Tech transfer success stories
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Process validation
- Manufacturing support

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Generic "To Whom It May Concern" greetings
❌ Repeating your resume verbatim
❌ Focusing only on what you want (not what you offer)
❌ Typos or grammar errors (especially company name!)
❌ Making it too long (keep to 1 page)
❌ Not customizing for each role
❌ Forgetting to attach it to the application!

✅ Do This Instead:
✅ Research and use hiring manager's name when possible
✅ Tell a story about your achievements
✅ Show enthusiasm for the specific company and role
✅ Proofread multiple times
✅ Keep it concise and impactful
✅ Customize each letter
✅ Save as PDF before submitting
