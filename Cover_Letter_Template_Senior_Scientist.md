## Customization Checklist

Before sending ANY cover letter, customize these elements:

### Research Required (30-45 minutes per company):
- [ ] **Company mission/vision** - Check "About Us" page
- [ ] **Recent news** - Press releases, funding rounds, clinical milestones (last 6 months)
- [ ] **Pipeline** - What therapeutic areas? What stage assets?
- [ ] **Technology platform** - Any unique approaches or proprietary tech?
- [ ] **Company culture** - LinkedIn posts, Glassdoor, employee interviews
- [ ] **Hiring manager** - LinkedIn research if name is known
- [ ] **Recent publications/presentations** - Google Scholar, company blog

### Required Replacements:
- [ ] Replace **[Company Name]** throughout (appears 8-12 times)
- [ ] Replace **[Hiring Manager Name]** or use "Hiring Committee" if unknown
- [ ] Replace **[Date]** with actual date
- [ ] Replace **[Specific Department/Area]** with exact job title
- [ ] Replace **[Company Address]** if sending physical mail (rare)

### Content Customization:
- [ ] Add **specific company achievement** (recent clinical data, partnership, etc.)
- [ ] Match **2-3 key skills** from job description
- [ ] Include **therapeutic area** or **modality** if specified in job posting
- [ ] Reference **specific technology platform** if company has one (e.g., ADC platform, mRNA, cell therapy)
- [ ] Adjust **tone** based on company culture (formal for big pharma, enthusiastic for startup)

### Final Checks:
- [ ] Spell-check and grammar check
- [ ] Verify company name spelling (Bristol-Myers Squibb vs Bristol Myers Squibb)
- [ ] Confirm contact info is current
- [ ] Keep to **1 page** (3-4 paragraphs max for Templates 1-2, up to 5 for Template 3)
- [ ] Save as **PDF**: `GirishSwayambhu_CoverLetter_[CompanyName]_[Date].pdf`

## Pro Tips for Cover Letters

### DO:
✅ **Customize every single letter** - Generic letters are obvious and rejected
✅ **Show you researched the company** - Reference specific programs, recent news
✅ **Lead with your strongest achievement** - $1M batch recovery is powerful
✅ **Match language from job description** - If they say "purification," use "purification" not "downstream processing"
✅ **Be specific with metrics** - "90% reduction" beats "significant improvement"
✅ **Show enthusiasm** - Especially for biotech; they want passionate team members
✅ **Address gaps proactively** - If changing focus areas, explain why (Template 4)

### DON'T:
❌ **Repeat resume verbatim** - Cover letter adds context and personality
❌ **Use generic opening** - "I am writing to apply..." is weak; lead with enthusiasm
❌ **Exceed 1 page** - Recruiters spend 30-60 seconds on cover letters
❌ **Focus on what you want** - Focus on what you can contribute
❌ **Use clichés** - "Team player," "hard worker," "detail-oriented" are empty
❌ **Send without customization** - Leaving [Company Name] brackets is instant rejection
❌ **Apologize or sound uncertain** - "I believe I might be a good fit..." → "I'm confident I can contribute..."

## Final Note

**The best cover letter is authentic and specific.** Use these templates as frameworks, but make sure your genuine interest in the company and role comes through. Research the company thoroughly, understand their challenges, and articulate how you can help solve them.

**Response rate data:**
- Generic cover letter: ~2-5% response rate
- Customized cover letter with company research: ~15-25% response rate
- Customized cover letter + referral: ~40-60% response rate

**Time investment:**
- Template selection: 2 minutes
- Company research: 30-45 minutes
- Customization: 15-30 minutes
- **Total: ~1 hour per application**

This time investment is worth it—quality applications (10 highly customized) outperform quantity (50 generic applications).

Good luck! 🚀
