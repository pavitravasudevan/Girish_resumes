## Job Description

This position involves leading complex pharmaceutical development projects at Thermo Fisher Scientific's Steriles plant. The role focuses on "formulation development, process optimization, and technology transfer" while maintaining GMP compliance standards.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Provide technical support for Formulation, Filling, and Freeze Drying operations
- Lead technical/scientific projects implementing innovative solutions
- Design processes and equipment for clinical, scale-up, and registration batches
- Conduct technology transfer from client projects
- Independently manage complex technical initiatives
- Support manufacturing operations and resolve CAPAs as a subject matter expert
- Develop proposals and lead client meetings
- Review technical documents, protocols, and bid quotes

---

## Required Qualifications

- Advanced degree plus 3 years experience OR Bachelor's degree plus 5 years pharmaceutical industry experience
- Expertise in HPLC, GC, LC/MS, and spectroscopy techniques
- Strong GMP and pharmaceutical regulatory knowledge
- Method development and validation experience
- Ability to manage multiple complex projects simultaneously
- Excellent technical documentation and communication skills
- Data analysis and statistical evaluation capabilities
- Physical capability: standing/walking, lifting up to 35 lbs, lab work dexterity

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biology, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or Engineering background
- Contract Manufacturing Organization (CMO) experience
- Aseptic techniques and sterile manufacturing knowledge
- Team development experience through technical guidance
