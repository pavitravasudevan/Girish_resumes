## Job Description

The role focuses on developing and optimizing manufacturing processes for **oral solid dosage forms** (tablets, capsules). The scientist will "lead and assist in developing, optimizing, and validating innovative manufacturing processes" with emphasis on **continuous manufacturing** techniques.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop, scale up, and optimize OSD manufacturing processes for continuous and batch operations
- Design and execute experiments using statistical and data science tools to enhance process performance
- Serve as technical lead in client discussions regarding process decisions
- Lead technology transfers from R&D to commercial manufacturing with compliance focus
- Collaborate with quality, operations, validation, and analytical teams
- Apply emerging technologies to drive innovation and efficiency
- Maintain regulatory, safety, and quality standard compliance

---

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in chemical engineering, chemistry, pharmacy, or related field with 4+ years experience in **oral solid dose manufacturing**; OR Master's degree with 2+ years experience; OR **Ph.D.**
- Manufacturing experience in product or process development
- Knowledge of GMP, process validation, and regulatory requirements
- **Proficiency in Python or MATLAB programming languages**
- Understanding of statistical tools for process development
- Strong problem-solving, communication, and project management skills

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- **Advanced knowledge of continuous manufacturing**
- Experience with immediate and controlled-release dosage forms

## Detailed Gap Analysis

### 1. Technical Domain Mismatch (Most Critical)

**Your World: Liquid Injectable Drug Substance**
- Proteins in solution (mAbs, bispecifics)
- Viral vectors in buffer (AAVs)
- Small molecule APIs in solvent
- **Purification:** Chromatography, filtration, crystallization
- **Unit operations:** Mixing, centrifugation, UFDF, drying (solution → powder)
- **Challenges:** Protein stability, aggregation, HCP clearance
- **Analytics:** DLS, mass spec, HPLC for purity

**This Role's World: Solid Oral Drug Product**
- Powders compressed into tablets or filled into capsules
- **Formulation:** Excipient selection, binder optimization, lubricant selection
- **Unit operations:** Granulation, milling, blending, compression, coating
- **Challenges:** Powder flow, tablet hardness, dissolution rate, content uniformity
- **Analytics:** Dissolution testing, friability, hardness, disintegration

**There is almost ZERO overlap between these worlds.**

---

### 2. Continuous Manufacturing Experience Gap

**Your Experience:**
- Batch chromatography (ÄKTA systems)
- Batch reactions and isolations
- Traditional batch processing mindset

**This Role Needs:**
- **Continuous manufacturing systems:**
  - Continuous granulation
  - Continuous blending
  - Continuous compression (high-speed tablet presses)
- Real-time release testing (RTRT)
- Process analytical technology (PAT)
- **Fundamentally different:**
  - Batch = make one lot, test, release
  - Continuous = material constantly flowing, real-time monitoring and control

This is a **highly specialized** area of pharmaceutical manufacturing. Most pharma scientists don't have this experience.
